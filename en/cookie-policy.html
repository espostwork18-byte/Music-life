1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites send to the user's device, where they are stored and then sent back to the same websites on the next visit. There are technical cookies, needed for a website to work, and other types of cookies (analytics, profiling) which normally require the user's consent.

2. Cookies used by this website

This website does not set profiling cookies or third-party cookies and does not use analytics or marketing tools. No external services are used to display fonts, scripts or content: all the resources needed are served from the website's own server.
